## About the Laboratory

The **System Intelligence Laboratory (SIL)** develops intelligent systems that can perceive, reason, decide, and act in complex real-world environments. Our research connects artificial intelligence with physical and biological systems, with a particular focus on **robotics**, **autonomous driving**, and **bio-manufacturing automation**.

We investigate learning-based perception, multimodal reasoning, planning, control, process prediction, optimization, and on-device AI deployment. Our goal is to build reliable and efficient intelligent systems that create measurable impact in mobility, robotics, and bio-manufacturing.

## Research Activities

| Research Area | Description |
|---|---|
| **Robotics and Physical AI** | Multimodal robot perception, vision-language-action models, scene understanding, manipulation, trajectory planning, motion control, and simulation-to-real deployment. |
| **Autonomous Driving and Intelligent Mobility** | End-to-end autonomous driving, BEV perception, deep reinforcement learning, generative and diffusion models, trajectory planning, vehicle control, safety, and on-device automotive AI. |
| **Bio-Manufacturing Automation** | AI-based cell imaging and sensing, real-time cell-culture monitoring, process prediction and optimization, digital twins, automated bioreactor operation, and embedded AI for bio-manufacturing systems. |
